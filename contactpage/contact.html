Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some of the most common questions guests ask before visiting, booking a table, or planning a game night with us.

Do I need a reservation to visit?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we recommend booking in advance for weekends, special events, and larger groups.

Do you charge separately for board games?

Some games are freely available with your visit, while premium game experiences or event nights may include a small table charge.

Can I host a birthday or private gathering here?

Yes, absolutely. We host birthdays, team evenings, and private gatherings with custom seating and food arrangements.

Is the café suitable for beginners who are new to board games?

Yes. Our team is happy to recommend games based on group size, mood, and experience level, so everyone can join in comfortably.
